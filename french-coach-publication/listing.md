## Short description

Practice natural, accurate everyday French with concise, useful feedback.

## Long description

French Coach helps learners express themselves more naturally and confidently in everyday French. It corrects important mistakes, distinguishes required corrections from optional stylistic improvements, and preserves the learner's intended meaning, tone, and register.

Use it to practice conversation, improve grammar and vocabulary, translate naturally from English or Spanish, understand why a phrase sounds unusual, create targeted exercises, or review recurring patterns. Feedback stays practical and concise by default, with more detail available when requested.

French Coach is designed for language practice and writing support. It does not certify proficiency and may make mistakes. Important legal, medical, immigration, academic, or professional wording should be reviewed by a qualified human.

## Key capabilities

- Correct genuine grammar and vocabulary errors.
- Make understandable but unnatural French more idiomatic.
- Preserve meaning, tone, and register.
- Translate naturally from English or Spanish.
- Continue realistic conversations in French.
- Explain useful grammar with short examples.
- Generate targeted exercises without revealing answers immediately.
- Review recurring strengths and improvement priorities.

## Starter prompts

### English

- Correct this French message and preserve my casual tone: “Je suis très excitée pour te voir demain.”
- Let’s have a conversation in French. Correct only the mistakes that matter.
- Translate this into natural French and give me a casual alternative: “I’m looking forward to the weekend.”

### Español

- Corrige este mensaje en francés y conserva mi tono informal: « Je suis très excitée pour te voir demain. »
- Practiquemos una conversación en francés. Corrige brevemente solo los errores importantes.
- Traduce “Tengo muchas ganas de que llegue el fin de semana” a un francés natural.

### Français

- Corrige ce message sans changer mon intention ni mon registre.
- Faisons une conversation naturelle et corrige brièvement mes erreurs importantes.
- Crée cinq exercices à partir de mes erreurs récentes sans donner les réponses.
